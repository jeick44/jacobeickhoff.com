The work

Six Engagements, each written the same way.

Every Engagement had a Sponsor, a scope and an outcome that was not mine to choose. Each one is written as a Case Study following the same five sections in the same order — The Situation, What I Did, What Changed, What I Learned, Where Else This Applies — so that two of them can be read against each other, and so that What I Learned reads as a method rather than an excuse.

  • Engagement 01

    Customer Intelligence

    A community bank in the Upper Midwest

    Browser-based mapping and profitability tools that gave leadership a spatial and demographic view of a customer base it had only ever seen as rows.

    Read the Case Study

  • Engagement 02

    Credit Process Rebuild

    A community bank in the Upper Midwest · ongoing

    Loan origination rebuilt as local-only browser tools: intake, tax-document extraction, spreads, and an approval presentation restructured into three separate stages.

    Read the Case Study
