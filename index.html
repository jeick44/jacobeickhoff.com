Engagement 01Customer Intelligence

A map of dots gets looked at. A number for the spot somebody pointed at gets argued with

Leadership had only ever seen its customer base as rows in an export. The tool I built turned those rows into a story they had never been able to see: point at an area, and see what it actually earns.

Why hovering shows the math. A color gives you the answer without the work. Hovering a location shows loan income minus deposit cost, so a disagreement lands on a specific number that can be checked.

Engagement 04ResearchFaculty-advised

The standard for success was set before any testing began, and finding nothing counts as an answer.

Whether AI-edited language in SEC filings predicts stock returns. The first question is whether that editing can be detected at all, so every detection method has to pass a fixed accuracy test before it’s used on a real filing.

Why it was rebuilt. I withdrew my own published first result after finding three defects in it. If no detector clears the bar, that is the finding, and the standard doesn’t move.

Engagement 06Regime & Sentiment AllocatorSelf-directed

An allocation process that can be replayed, argued with, and shown to be wrong.

A two-layer system built and run end to end by one person: it fetches, classifies the macro regime, allocates across fifteen funds, and writes itself a plain-English briefing, on a timer.

Why there is no return figure here. The history it can be tested over is fifty-six trading days. A ratio annualized from that describes the window, not the system, so this is published as architecture.

All six Engagements

  • Engagement 01

    Customer Intelligence

    A community bank in the Upper Midwest

    Browser-based mapping and profitability tools that gave leadership a spatial and demographic view of a customer base it had only ever seen as rows.

  • Engagement 02

    Credit Process Rebuild

    A community bank in the Upper Midwest · ongoing

    Loan origination rebuilt as local-only browser tools: intake, tax-document extraction, spreads, and an approval presentation restructured into three separate stages.

  • Engagement 03

    Hedge Fund Case Competition

    Four-person team

    Our paper, “Operational AI Adoption in a Winner-Take-Most Economy,” scored companies on how they describe putting AI to work, and reports the two earlier periods when the same signal pointed the wrong way.

  • Engagement 04

    Research

    Independent · faculty-advised

    Tests whether AI editing can be spotted at three strengths, light, moderate and heavy, before any detection method is allowed near a real filing and its returns.

  • Engagement 05

    Investment Decision Tools

    Self-directed · product design and architecture

    Reads the broker’s record and never places an order. What the investor did sits beside what they said they did, never averaged, because the gap between the two is the signal. A companion charting tool records what each chart showed.

  • Engagement 06

    Regime & Sentiment Allocator

    Self-directed

    Sorts the market into one of four regimes, allocates across fifteen sector and credit funds, and writes itself a plain-English briefing on a schedule. Fifty-six trading days is too short to judge returns, so it is published as a design.
