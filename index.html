About

A finance student who ships software, and an engineer who reads the credit file.

I am a Finance and Accounting student at UW–Eau Claire, and for the last two years I have been the person a community bank in the Upper Midwest hands an undocumented process to. I sit on both sides of it: I do the credit analysis, and I build the tooling that changes how the analysis gets done.

The work here is organised as Engagements: each one had a Sponsor, a scope and an outcome that was not mine to choose. Every one of them carries a What I Learned section — a defect found, an assumption that proved wrong, a perspective that changed. That section is the reason the rest is worth reading.

The work

Six Engagements, each written the same way.

One Sponsor, one scope, one outcome per Engagement — and the same five sections in the same order every time: The Situation, What I Did, What Changed, What I Learned, Where Else This Applies.

  • Engagement 01

    Customer Intelligence

    A community bank in the Upper Midwest

    Browser-based mapping and profitability tools that gave leadership a spatial and demographic view of a customer base it had only ever seen as rows.

  • Engagement 02

    Credit Process Rebuild

    A community bank in the Upper Midwest · ongoing

    Loan origination rebuilt as local-only browser tools: intake, tax-document extraction, spreads, and an approval presentation restructured into three separate stages.

  • Engagement 03

    The Competition

    Four-person team · hedge fund case competition

    “Operational AI Adoption in a Winner-Take-Most Economy” — reported alongside the two earlier periods in which the same signal pointed the wrong way.

  • Engagement 04

    The Research

    Independent · faculty-advised

    Whether AI-generated language in SEC filings predicts abnormal returns — rebuilt from the ground up after I withdrew my own first result.

  • Engagement 05

    TradeLog and Indy

    Self-directed

    Verified Adherence: holding a discretionary process to a rule written down in advance, and making the gap between the two visible.

  • Engagement 06

    MacroSense

    Self-directed

    A macro-signal architecture designed to be falsifiable before it was designed to be right, and published as architecture rather than as performance.
